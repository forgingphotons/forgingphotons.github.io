Resembling a cosmic Viking helmet crowned with sweeping, glowing wings of gas, NGC 2359—popularly known as Thor's Helmet—is one of the most dynamic and aggressive structures in the night sky.

At the violent heart of this complex nebula sits WR 7, an extremely rare and volatile Wolf-Rayet star. This star is an absolute stellar monster in the final, frantic stages of its life before an inevitable supernova explosion. Generating furious stellar winds moving at millions of kilometers per hour, WR 7 is violently stripping away its own outer layers.

What makes Thor's Helmet truly unique is a dramatic interaction: as this high-speed wind plows outward, it violently collides with a surrounding dense molecular cloud left over from an earlier phase of the star's evolution. This cosmic head-on crash creates shock-heated waves, intricate filaments, and bright blue-green arcs of oxygen and hydrogen gas, sculpting a magnificent and menacing bubble that looks ready for battle in the deep cosmos.
