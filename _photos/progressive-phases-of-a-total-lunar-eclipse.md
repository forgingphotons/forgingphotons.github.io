This striking multi-stage composite sequence traces the complete celestial journey of a Total Lunar Eclipse, capturing its progressive phases across the night sky. Rather than a single frame or a compressed time-lapse, this meticulously composed sequence freezes key milestones of the alignment, displaying the Moon's gradual transition from a brilliant full disk into a deep, coppery ember before returning to its full, unshaded brilliance.

The sequence begins with the subtle dimming of the penumbral entry, quickly followed by the sharp, crisp bite of Earth’s dark umbral shadow as it sweeps across the lunar craters and maria. As the Moon moves deeper into alignment, each successive frame reveals the encroaching curve of the shadow until totality is achieved. At the center of the arc, the Moon glows with the haunting, ethereal beauty of a "Blood Moon," illuminated entirely by sunlight filtered and refracted through the ring of Earth's atmosphere via Rayleigh scattering.

This sequential arrangement beautifully illustrates the precise orbital geometry and shadow mechanics governing our Earth-Moon system over the course of several hours of observation.
