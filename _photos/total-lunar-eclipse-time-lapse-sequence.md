This panoramic time-lapse sequence compresses the hours-long transformation of a Total Lunar Eclipse into a seamless visual journey. As Earth glides directly between the Sun and the Moon, the sequence traces every phase of the alignment—from the subtle dimming during penumbral entry to the dramatic sweep of Earth's dark umbral shadow across the lunar maria.

At the peak of totality, the full Moon undergoes its iconic transformation into a "Blood Moon." Completely immersed in Earth's shadow, the lunar surface is illuminated solely by sunlight filtered and refracted through the edges of Earth's atmosphere. Rayleigh scattering strips away the shorter blue wavelengths of sunlight, leaving a rich spectrum of deep copper, amber, and blood-red light to paint the cratered terrain.

By combining precise, exposure-bracketed frames across the entire event, this sequence reveals the stark change in brightness between the brilliant full Moon and its faint, crimson glow during totality, highlighting the cosmic geometry of our Sun-Earth-Moon system.
