Often referred to as the Diamond Ring Nebula, Abell 33 is a breathtaking display of cosmic geometry hidden deep within the constellation Hydra.

What makes this planetary nebula a truly unique and captivating subject is a stunning perspective coincidence: sitting right on the lower edge of its glowing gaseous shell is HD 83535, a bright foreground star. This accidental alignment creates the unmistakable illusion of a cosmic engagement ring, where the nebula forms the band and the star sparkles like a brilliant diamond set into it.

Upon closer inspection, Abell 33 also stands out for its remarkably near-perfect spherical shape. Unlike many other planetary nebulas shaped by complex interactions or chaotic stellar winds, the near-geometric symmetry of this bubble is the result of the central star's lack of rotation when it shed its outer layers. The final result is a delicate, symmetrical stellar relic floating in the silence of deep space.
