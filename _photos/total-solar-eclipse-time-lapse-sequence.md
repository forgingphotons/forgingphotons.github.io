This dynamic time-lapse sequence captures the continuous evolution of the Total Solar Eclipse of August 12, 2026, tracing its full progression over the historic sky of Zaragoza. From first contact—as the silhouette of the Moon gradually bites into the solar disk—to the climax of totality and the eventual return of daylight, this composite tells the complete story of one of nature's most awe-inspiring spectacles.

As the Moon completely covers the Sun, the dramatic shift from brilliant daylight to atmospheric twilight unfolds in seconds. The sequence highlights the fleeting appearance of the pearly-white solar corona, magnetic plasma loops, and the famous "diamond ring" effect marking the exact boundaries of totality. By compressing time across multiple frames, this piece reveals the smooth, clockwork precision of orbital mechanics and the breathtaking transformation of the sky above.
